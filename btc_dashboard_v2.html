Methodology & badge rules
  • Rows: the 12 completed calendar months plus the current partial month. Historical cells use the last valid observation within the month (monthly M2: that month's observation). The current row shows each metric's latest published observation, with its own date.
  • NLM 8W Δ: change over 8 weeks in net liquidity = WALCL/1e6 − WDTGAL/1e6 − RRPONTSYD/1,000 ($T), with all three on the same Wednesday. If any input is missing for that Wednesday (e.g. a holiday), the value is unavailable and is not carried forward. This is an analytical proxy, not a proven lead indicator.
  • Bullish: M2 3-month change > +0.1% · NLM > +0.1T · RRP < $500B · TGA < $600B · Real yield < 1.5% · WALCL 13-week change > 0
  • Bearish: M2 < −0.1% · NLM < −0.1T · RRP > $1,000B · TGA > $800B · Real yield > 2.5% · WALCL < −3%. Neutral: in between.
  • L1 Signal: Bullish if ≥4 of 6 bullish; Bearish if ≥3 bearish; otherwise Neutral. If missing inputs could change the result, it shows Insufficient data. L1 score: bull 2 / neutral 1 / bear 0 points averaged over available inputs (needs ≥4).
  • Historical values are FRED's latest revised observations, not real-time vintages. Use ALFRED for backtests. Click any value to see its source.

Dashboard Overview

What This Dashboard Does

It reads Bitcoin's environment in three layers:

  • L1 — Macro liquidity: US M2, net liquidity, the Fed balance sheet, Treasury cash (TGA), reverse repo and real yields.
  • L2 — Market pressure & flow: the US dollar (DXY), the 10Y−2Y yield curve and BTC versus the Nasdaq-100 (QQQ).
  • L3 — Crypto internals: MVRV, BTC dominance, the stablecoin supply ratio and perpetual funding.

Each layer scores 0–10 from the inputs that have data, and the composite is their average (at least two layers are needed). Action bands:

  • ≥ 8 Strong Buy
  • ≥ 6.5 Cautious Accumulate
  • ≥ 5 Hold / Small DCA
  • ≥ 3 Reduce Exposure
  • < 3 Exit / Cash

How to Read Historical Tables

The Layer 1 table shows the last 12 completed months plus the current month. Each cell is a real FRED observation with its badge, and you can click it for the exact source and date. Look for runs of the same colour rather than single months:

  • M2 rising (green trend) means more money in the system. A lead of 8–12 weeks over BTC is often quoted, but it is a hypothesis, not something this dashboard has verified.
  • RRP falling means cash leaving the Fed's reverse-repo facility and entering markets. Near zero, there is little left to release.
  • DXY falling (Layer 2 chart, not in the table) means a weaker US dollar, which has historically been a tailwind for BTC.

"Not yet published" means the data isn't due yet, not that it is missing. Values are FRED's latest revised figures, not what was known at the time.

Why Only a Few Charts?

For Layer 1, the table replaced the six sparklines, including M2 and RRP. A table cell gives the exact value, its observation date and its signal colour for every month, which a small line cannot show reliably.

Charts are kept only where a real history feed exists: BTC/USDT (180 days), DXY, the 10Y−2Y spread, BTC/QQQ and funding rates. MVRV, BTC dominance and SSR show the current value only, because no free source provides their history. The dashboard does not draw sample lines in their place.

Help & Parameters

Weights: within each layer, every input counts equally (bullish 2, neutral 1, bearish 0 points). If an input has no data, the remaining inputs share its weight. The badges use the thresholds below; Layer 2 and 3 scores use slightly finer steps, shown in brackets.

Bearish Signal

L3 Layer 3 — Crypto Internals (4 inputs, needs ≥ 3)

▪ MVRV Z-Score
What It Is
How far BTC's market value sits above its "realized" value (the price coins last moved at), in standard deviations. You enter it by hand, because no verified free automated source exists.
Bullish Signal
Below 0.5 (accumulate zone; score steps: < 0 / < 1.5 / < 3)
Bearish Signal
3 or above (take profits); 6 or above (exit zone)
Weight
25% of Layer 3 score (1 of 4); excluded until you enter a value
▪ BTC Dominance
What It Is
BTC's share of total crypto market cap (CoinGecko). High dominance = capital favouring BTC; falling dominance = rotation into alts.
Bullish Signal
Above 58% (score steps: > 58 / > 55 / > 50)
Bearish Signal
Below 50% (altseason)
Weight
25% of Layer 3 score (1 of 4)
▪ Stablecoin Supply Ratio (SSR)
What It Is
BTC market cap divided by the total stablecoin market cap. A low SSR means a lot of "dry powder" relative to BTC.
Bullish Signal
SSR below 8 (score steps: < 7 / < 10 / < 13)
Bearish Signal
SSR 12 or above (distribution)
Weight
25% of Layer 3 score (1 of 4)
▪ Funding Rates (Open Interest not tracked)
What It Is
The 8-hour fee between long and short traders on Binance's BTCUSDT perpetual futures. Positive = longs pay (crowded longs); negative = shorts pay. Open interest is not part of this dashboard.
Bullish Signal
Below −0.005% per 8h, shorts crowded / squeeze setup (score steps: < −0.02 / < 0 / < 0.02%)
Bearish Signal
0.01% per 8h or above, crowded longs
Weight
25% of Layer 3 score (1 of 4)
